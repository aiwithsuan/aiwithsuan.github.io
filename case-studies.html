Key Lessons Learned Across 12 Years

  • Data Integrity Precedes AI Success: Machine learning and AI agents are only as good as the underlying data lineage. Resolving identity fragmentation at the schema level is the single highest-leverage investment an enterprise can make.
  • Bridge the Human-Technical Divide: The best systems integration architecture fails if operational teams don't trust it. Active collaboration with process engineers, site supervisors, and IT leads to true adoption.
  • Design for Scale from Day One: Building data pipelines with modularity, clean reference data governance, and high availability prevents costly technical debt when scaling across global facilities.
