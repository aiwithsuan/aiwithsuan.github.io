My Professional Story

My career has been a continuous journey of learning, adapting, and creating value through technology. Starting from software engineering, I expanded into enterprise systems integration, global manufacturing analytics, data warehousing, and modern analytics engineering. Across three countries and multiple industries, my work centers on building the Digital Thread—unifying fragmented systems to establish complete data traceability. My core purpose has remained consistent: Using technology and data to help organizations make better decisions.
